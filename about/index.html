About

Savanna Guides is your companion to exploring the wild beauty of East Africa. Built for hikers, overlanders, and adventure seekers, our platform brings together detailed guides to national parks, trails, and unique destinations across Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, and Rwanda.

We make it easier to plan your journeys with curated itineraries, gear checklists, and insider tips—whether you’re heading out for a weekend hike, a multi-day trek, or a cross-country road trip.

At its heart, Savanna Guides is about connecting people with nature. We want you to experience the awe of African landscapes, the thrill of discovery, and the freedom of the open road—all while having the practical tools you need at your fingertips.

From seasoned adventurers to first-time explorers, Savanna Guides is here to help you get out there, explore responsibly, and make the most of every journey.

Adventure your way

Choose the path that fits your style. Whether you’re a hiker, an overlander, or a casual explorer, Savanna Guides offers flexible tools and curated ideas to match your adventure goals..

Plan with confidence

No more guesswork—our itineraries, gear checklists, and practical tips make it easy to prepare for your journey. From short hikes to multi-day expeditions, you’ll always know what to expect and how to get ready.

Built for explorers, by explorers.

Savanna Guides was created by outdoor enthusiasts who understand the challenges and joys of adventure in East Africa. Everything we share comes from real experience and a passion for the outdoors..
