Questions

Before you download

Is Savanna Guides free?

Yes. You can download the app and use the free tier at no cost. A premium tier is also available.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. On the free tier, you can download trails and use them without signal. Park downloads are part of Premium.

Which countries does it cover?

Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda.

What is in the guide?

45 parks, 36 trails, 41 campsites and 329 places to stay, with a field guide to 136 animals and 1,602 birds.

Is it available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.
